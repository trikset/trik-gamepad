TRIK Gamepad turns your device into a wireless gamepad for TRIK robots. Connect over Wi-Fi, control the robot with touch pads and buttons, and watch a live video stream from the robot's camera.

Features:
- Dual touch pads with steering wheel and accelerometer control modes
- Hardware gamepad button support
- Configurable magic buttons for custom robot commands
- Live video streaming (MJPEG) from the robot camera
- UDP and TCP control protocols
- Custom message commands for user programs
- Connection diagnostics with crash reporting
- System bar auto-hide in gamepad mode

Available in English, Russian, French, German, and Vietnamese.
